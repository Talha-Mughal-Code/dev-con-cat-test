# Evaluation Rubric

This is intentionally shared with candidates. We'd rather everyone optimize for
the things that actually matter than guess. Total 100 points; the weighting tells
you where to spend your 3–5 days.

## Scoring

### 1. Domain modeling & architecture — 25 pts
- Clear, sensible models for accounts, users, pixels, leads, verification runs,
  layer results, verdicts, and certificates.
- The three layer states (not-enabled / not-applicable / returned-verdict) are
  represented cleanly, not conflated.
- Boundaries make sense (services/jobs/policies vs. fat models or fat
  controllers). Reasoning is explained in `SOLUTION.md`.

### 2. Consensus engine — 20 pts
- Combines all layer "voices" into `ACCEPT / REVIEW / REJECT` **with a reason**.
- Distinguishes hard stops (litigator, DNC, consent mismatch, exact duplicate)
  from weighted signals (a single disagreeing provider, "suspect", VPN).
- Handles unavailable/errored layers deliberately (fail-open vs fail-closed).
- Behaviour is defensible against the 12 seed leads (not hardcoded to the hints).

### 3. Multi-tenancy & authorization — 15 pts
- Account isolation enforced at the **query layer**, not just hidden in the UI.
- Roles (`super_admin`, `account_admin`, `member`) properly enforced.
- Demonstrable: a member of account A cannot reach account B's data by any route.

### 4. Credits, subscriptions & super-admin — 10 pts
- Credits consumed on a defensible rule; accounting is correct.
- "Out of credits mid-verification" has a defined, sensible behaviour.
- Super-admin dashboard gives a real cross-account overview (plans, balances,
  burn rate, who's about to run dry). Flags the past-due near-zero account.

### 5. Pixel + real-time landing page — 15 pts
- Pixel snippet is generated by the app and scoped to an account.
- Ingestion endpoint accepts leads and can't be used to write into another
  account.
- The example landing page is **wired to the real backend** so activity displays
  live as the form is filled/submitted — not the simulation. Transport choice is
  justified.

### 6. Consent certificates — 8 pts
- A certificate is issued per processed lead, captures the evidence a buyer would
  need to defend it, references the retained TrustedForm cert, and is
  retrievable/verifiable. Tamper-evidence considered.

### 7. Craft: tests, docs, reasoning — 7 pts
- Meaningful tests on the highest-value logic (consensus engine, auth
  boundaries).
- `SOLUTION.md` answers `docs/DESIGN_QUESTIONS.md` clearly and honestly names
  gaps and next steps.
- Runs from the README with the provided seed data.

## Signals that stand out (positive)
- The candidate can explain *why*, not just *what*, in their own words.
- Policy (weights/thresholds) is configurable/data-driven with a clear rationale.
- Honest trade-offs and an explicit "here's what I cut and why" section.
- Thoughtful handling of borderline leads (soft duplicate, suspected litigator,
  provider disagreement) rather than crude pass/fail.

## Red flags (negative)
- Verdicts hardcoded to the `expected_verdict` hints.
- Tenant isolation only enforced in views; data leaks via direct IDs/params.
- The "real-time" page is still the bundled simulation.
- A giant, unfocused app that doesn't run, or that blew way past the timebox.
- Can't defend core decisions in the follow-up interview.

## Interview follow-up
We'll walk through your `SOLUTION.md` and ask you to defend the consensus engine,
your isolation strategy, and your credit model live. Build what you can explain.
